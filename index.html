They launch the founder, not the product.

Every launch Social Capital Inc lists publicly, its hero post, and the creator wave that follows, rebuilt from public X data and set against nine launches that ran without them.

The finding

Social Capital does not amplify the product. It amplifies the founder's life story, on a schedule, through a small recurring roster.

The hero post sits on the founder's personal account in seven of nine launches. The highest-reach posts in the wave are not product reviews but greentext biographies of the founder, written in the same voice across launches by the same handful of accounts. The wave has two peaks: a lore burst in the first day, then a product-angle burst on day five, often landing inside a three-hour window.

    Reach does not follow the founder's follower count

    Views on the hero post divided by the author's followers. A launch with no distribution network usually gets 5 to 50 views per follower. Social Capital launches from accounts with 7,000 to 40,000 followers get 100 to 400. The gap is the network.

    The launch clock

    When each hero post went live. Eight of nine Social Capital posts land on a Monday or Tuesday, and all nine between 13:00 and 18:00 UTC, which is mid-morning in New York and evening in India. The comparison set is spread across the week. They front-load the week so the wave has Tuesday to Friday to run.

    Method and limits

    Launch list

    The nine case studies on sociallcapital.com/work as of 15 September 2026. Each page embeds one hero post, and that post is the anchor for everything else.

    Metrics

    From the public fxtwitter API, which returns views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes, bookmarks, author followers and video length without a login. Numbers are as of the date under the hero, so older launches have had longer to accumulate.

    Wave posts

    Found with X search restricted to a date window after each hero post, the product or founder name, and 20 or more likes. X returns one reliable page per query, so each wave is a sample of the most engaged posts, not a census. Wispr Flow also merges URLs from a public dataset another applicant compiled (waveline-socap); every post was re-fetched and re-checked here.

    Comparison set

    Nine AI launch posts from company or founder accounts with no known agency: Cluely, Manus, Devin and Cursor. Picked for being well known, not for matching size. Views per follower is the fair comparison; raw views are not.

    What "founder biography" means

    A post whose body is greentext lines beginning with "be", the 4chan-style life story format. Detected by pattern, then read by hand.

    What this cannot show

    Whether any account was paid. Which posts were written in-house. LinkedIn, where Social Capital says most of its reach is, is not publicly searchable. The roster overlap and the biography pattern are visible in public data; the mechanism behind them is inferred.
